Reads. Never writes. Reads. Never writes.

Claude Meter uses the sign‑in Claude Code already saved on your Mac. It reads it from the macOS Keychain, through Apple’s own security tool, or from ~/.claude/.credentials.json, and never changes it. Short‑lived tokens are refreshed in memory, never written back. Up to 24 hours of readings, kept for the sparklines and forecast, stay on your Mac.
